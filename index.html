About me

I’m a Women in AI Fellowship fellow training as an AI/ML engineer. I build and evaluate models in Python and work with databases in SQL. I’m also an Assistant Lecturer at the Nigerian Army College of Education and a certified educationist, so I know how quickly a child decides “I’m not a math person.”

That is the problem I want to solve. I’m building toward work in AI/ML engineering, AI automation, AI ethics and governance, and EdTech research and product, with a focus on Africa and Nigeria.

I’m faith-forward, community-minded, and happiest when I’m helping a young person find their path.
